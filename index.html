Keyboard Shortcuts Tutorial

This tutorial teaches you keyboard shortcuts to move through your computer faster and more efficiently — things like switching between tabs, copying and pasting, screenshotting, and other ways to navigate without relying on your mouse as much. I went through each page and tried out all the commands as I went. Some I already knew, but a lot were completely new to me. The tutorial also had practice exercises, like copying text from one page, switching to another window, and pasting it there, or practicing refreshing a page using just the keyboard. By the end, I felt a lot quicker moving around my computer and switching between windows without having to click around as much.

100,000 Stars - https://stars.chromeexperiments.com/

This project is a zoomed-in visualization of stars and galaxies that lets you explore space right from your browser. I thought this one was so cool because I've never seen anything like it presented before, and I don't know of any other website that shows something like this. It's remarkable to me just because it's a completely new way of looking at something I'd never really been able to visualize before.

Pointer - https://pointer.playables.net/

This website is very simple but very cool — as you move your cursor around, it kind of moves everything you touch in a certain direction, similar to those two-sided satisfying pillows where each side has a different image. I thought it was mesmerizing and cool how they took a real-life kind of fidget toy and turned it into something you can experience online. It's also really interesting to think about the coding behind something like that.
